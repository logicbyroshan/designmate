Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries about our services

What is the minimum order quantity?

We accept orders starting from as low as 50 cards. However, bulk orders of 500+ cards get special discounts and priority processing.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 7-10 working days. Express delivery is available in 3-5 days at an additional cost. For urgent orders, we offer 48-hour rush service.

Can I get custom designs for my school?

Absolutely! We offer completely custom design services. Our design team will work with you to create unique ID cards that match your school's branding and requirements.

What materials do you use for cards?

We offer multiple material options including PVC cards, eco-friendly materials, paper laminated cards, and smart cards with RFID chips. All materials are high-quality and durable.

Do you provide samples before bulk orders?

Yes, we provide digital proofs for approval before printing. For new clients, we can also send physical samples of our previous work to demonstrate quality.

What if there are errors in the printed cards?

We have a strict quality control process. If any errors occur on our end, we reprint the affected cards at no additional cost. For data errors from the client side, we offer discounted reprints.
